Parallel Humans

Red Pill

There are some characters that do not belong to anything on earth and do not belong to our universe. We call them parallel symbols. We are dealing with parallel humans, and we indeed know almost nothing about them. We have no idea what they look like, how they think, or what kind of world they live in. We know almost nothing about these most fundamental and essential things.

If we were merely dolls in the hands of those parallel humans in historical research, then this research could not have been published here. We will neither belittle humanity and human wisdom, nor regard parallel humans as omnipotent gods.
